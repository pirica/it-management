Personal Emergency Plan (PEP)

This example document explains how the hotel supports guests who declare accessibility needs during online booking.

During your stay

  • Staff are trained to assist with evacuation routes suited to mobility, hearing, visual, or cognitive needs when declared at check-in.
  • Visual alarms and tactile signage are available in accessible rooms and public routes where configured.
  • Room controls and layouts follow high-contrast and simplified patterns where requested.

In an emergency

  • Follow staff instructions and posted evacuation maps.
  • If you require assistance, contact the front desk or dial the emergency number shown in your room.
  • Service animals accompanying a declared accessibility need are permitted in accordance with property policy.

Customize this HTML file per property and set the URL in Hotel Booking Settings → Accessibility PEP URL.
